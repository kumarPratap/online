About Pratap's Cosmos

Welcome to Pratap's Cosmos — my personal space on the web for learning, building, exploring, and documenting the things that interest me.

About Me

I am Pratap Kumar, an Electrical Engineer with a growing interest in Computer Science, technology, and web development. I am currently pursuing my B.Tech in Computer Science and using this website as part of my journey to learn by building.

My interests are spread across electrical engineering, Computer Science, artificial intelligence, technology, web development, and creative work. I enjoy understanding how things work and experimenting with ideas rather than simply learning them theoretically.

Why Pratap's Cosmos?

I created this website as a personal digital space where I can bring together different parts of my learning and interests. Instead of keeping everything scattered across different places, I wanted to build a space that I can continuously develop and make my own.

The word Cosmos represents the idea of a space where different interests, ideas, projects, experiments, and discoveries can exist together.

My Learning Journey

I am currently building my foundation in Computer Science and web development. I started with HTML and am now learning CSS, responsive design, and how to structure a multi-page website properly.

My next step is to learn JavaScript and gradually move towards building more interactive and useful web applications.

I believe in learning the fundamentals first and then using more advanced tools when I understand why they are needed.

Engineering & Technology

Electrical engineering remains an important part of my background. I am interested in finding connections between electrical engineering, Computer Science, programming, and technology.

In the future, I would like to build useful digital tools, calculators, references, and learning resources that combine engineering knowledge with software.

Learning by Building

Pratap's Cosmos is itself one of my learning projects. I am not trying to make it perfect from the beginning. Instead, I am improving it gradually as I learn new concepts.

Every new section, layout problem, CSS experiment, and design decision is an opportunity to understand something better.

Beyond Technology

This website is not only about Computer Science and engineering. I also have an interest in music, poetry, creativity, and exploration.

These interests have their own place in the website because I believe a personal website should represent more than just professional skills.

Looking Ahead

I want Pratap's Cosmos to grow alongside me. As I learn more, the website will gradually include better projects, learning notes, experiments, engineering resources, creative work, and useful tools.

I don't know exactly what this website will look like years from now, and that is part of the idea. It is meant to evolve with my knowledge, interests, and experiences.

Learn by building. Explore by questioning. Improve by experimenting.

A Work in Progress

This website is a work in progress — just like my learning journey. Things will change, pages will be improved, projects will be completed, and new ideas will appear.

Thanks for visiting my little corner of the web.
