Why I Built This Website

I built this site to have my own open canvas on the web — without algorithms, feeds, or corporate templates.

I believe the best way to understand anything is to build it by hand. Making this site from scratch with pure HTML and CSS taught me more about design, layouts, and web fundamentals than just reading tutorials.

It gives me a personal place to showcase projects I'm working on, share real nature photos I take, and keep notes on things I care about.

Build things with your own hands. You'll understand them much deeper.
