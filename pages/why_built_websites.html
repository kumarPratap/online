Why I Built This Website

I built this website because I believe the most enduring way to learn is by building real things from scratch.

I created this website to document my journey in technology, Computer Science, and electrical engineering. It is a home where I can share my projects, experiments, notes, and the lessons learned along the way.

Every page reflects curiosity and continuous refinement. Some experiments are simple, while others are more involved, but each one marks progress in understanding fundamental systems.

This website also serves as my living digital portfolio — showcasing skills, craft, and a passion for lifelong learning.

Thank you for visiting. I hope you find something useful, interesting, or inspiring here.
