How I Learn

I learn best by combining theory with practice. Instead of only reading about a technology, I try to build something with it and learn from the problems I encounter.

  1. Understand the basic concept.
  2. Study how it works under the hood.
  3. Build something small.
  4. Experiment, test, and debug.
  5. Document the findings.
  6. Build something more complete and useful.
Learn the fundamentals. Build something. Understand why it works.
