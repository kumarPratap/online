Why This Website is Simple

Most of the internet today is filled with popups, banners, and heavy trackers. I wanted this website to feel like opening a clean book — calm, fast, and easy to read.

Fast & Lightweight

It is built with pure HTML and CSS. It loads instantly on any phone or slow mobile connection without heavy JavaScript libraries slowing it down.

Zero Clutter

No cookie popups, no ads, no trackers, and no distracting notifications. Just honest writing, real photos, and useful projects.

Easy to Maintain

Because it uses standard web technologies, it will never break due to framework updates or build errors. It is built to last.

Simplicity is not about having less — it is about making room for what actually matters.
