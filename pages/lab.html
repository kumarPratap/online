The Lab

Welcome to the Lab — an experimental space where I test out new ideas, layout techniques, CSS styling experiments, and small prototypes.

Not every experiment is meant to be a full project. The Lab is where I explore concepts freely, break things, understand how they work, and refine them.

Current Experiments

  • Scholarly Navigation: Clean translucent headers and responsive cards with minimal borders.
  • Embedded Media Layouts: Responsive Spotify and YouTube iframe containers with aspect-ratio preservation.
