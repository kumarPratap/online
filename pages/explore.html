Questions I'm Curious About

Some questions don't have to be answered immediately. Sometimes the question itself is interesting enough to explore.

  • Why do some technologies feel like magic until you look underneath them?
  • Why do I keep coming back to music, poetry, and words when I’m trying to understand something I can’t quite explain?
  • What kind of person am I becoming through all the things I’m learning?
  • What would I build if I stopped worrying about whether it was “good enough”?
Stay curious. There is always something new to discover.
